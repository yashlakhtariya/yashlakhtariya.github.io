Offering Dandavat Pranam Toward the Sacred Dham

You can offer humble obeisances (Dandavat Pranam) facing the direction of your beloved Dham. Offering Pranam toward the Dham deepens your love and attachment for the sacred abode, and invokes the boundless grace and mercy of the Lord upon you. It is recommended to offer Dandavat Pranam toward the Dham three times a day—in the morning, at noon, and in the evening.
